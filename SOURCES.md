# Team Leader — design references

Team Leader contains written methods, blank project templates and Codex interface configuration. Its 21 skill files and one MIT license do not bundle third-party software, images, fonts or runtime dependencies.

Development records identify these conceptual influences:

- OpenAI's Astra guidance: instruction boundaries, professional autonomy, appropriate delegation and proportionate validation. Available models and tools depend on the host.
- Donella H. Meadows, *Thinking in Systems* and systems leverage points: reinforcing feedback can develop possibilities; balancing feedback compares results with goals and guides corrections.
- Chris Argyris, double-loop learning: examine methods and assumptions as well as correcting results.
- PDSA: check behavior and results after changing a method.
- Impeccable's approach to design critique: assess the whole experience rather than substituting mechanical checks for design judgment.

These are design references, not bundled dependencies or endorsements. This release did not conduct a complete literature review or line-by-line provenance audit. No bundled third-party assets were identified; this does not guarantee the provenance of every phrase.
