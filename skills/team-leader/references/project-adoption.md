# Project Adoption and Knowledge Structure

Use this reference when the user establishes this methodology in a new project, brings an existing project into it, or asks for a substantive takeover and reorganization. Discussion, a status question, or an ordinary fix does not by itself initialize or migrate a project.

## Required structure, flexible integration

Create a real, discoverable home for every area below. The requirement is the complete structure and its ownership, not a particular file count. Use the bundled layout for new projects; integrate existing useful files and trackers and add missing homes. Multiple areas may share clear sections, subject to the stable experience entry below. A link to a nonexistent destination does not establish a home.

| Required area | Default home | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Methodology rules and navigation | AGENTS.md and README.md | Lead; common rules, reading triggers, protocol and run/use entry points |
| Six professional responsibilities and task access | roles/ for responsibility text; docs/TEAM.md for index/interfaces; actual tasks in the overview | Lead coordinates authoritative role cards, navigation, and real task access |
| Product agreement | docs/PRODUCT.md | Product; intended understanding or action, effective scope, rules, and experience direction |
| Technical agreement | docs/TECHNICAL.md | Technical; reliable methods, evidence requirements, relevant architecture, data, interfaces, environment, and rationale |
| Acceptance agreement | docs/ACCEPTANCE.md | Product meaning with technical and independent reviewer contributions |
| Work items, progress, blockers, and next actions | PROGRESS.md; BLOCKED.md or a clear overview section | Lead; outcomes, relevant dependencies, responsibility, state, usable entries |
| Requirements changes and important decisions | reports/ or an existing issue/decision record | Responsible role with lead coordination; reason, authority, affected contracts, supersession |
| Production and verification evidence | reports/ or existing task/evidence records, linked to delivered versions | Producer and reviewer; meaningful results, self-checks, independent findings, limits |
| Project experience and handoff | Stable entry: docs/METHODOLOGY.md; linked detail and handoff/current-state records | Contributors with lead stewardship; applicable lessons and recoverable continuity |

Maintain one project map, normally in PROGRESS.md, with each area's actual file/section and responsible role. Map the six responsibilities to actual deliverables under [Identity and authority](protocol.md#identity-and-authority); adapt template fields to that work rather than inventing software architecture for research or services. Keep specific continuing responsibilities in their role cards, with concise TEAM navigation and shared coordination interfaces; retain the common protocol's cross-role authority and acceptance relationships. Preserve useful adopted methods and the current stage. README.md and AGENTS.md point to the map; actual task identities and current assignments stay in the overview rather than the role cards.

For projects adopting this workflow, keep the experience entry at `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` relative to the project root, directly linked from AGENTS.md and the authoritative overview. Help readers discover applicable lessons by situation or topic, using concise methods or direct links to their sole authoritative details and effective contracts under [Project knowledge](protocol.md#project-knowledge). Dated history can remain available but should not be the only route to current lessons; keep a useful existing organization without imposing a table or new directory. Existing operations, habits, and decision records may remain details where suitable; preserve unique content and provenance without duplicate authorities. During authorized adoption or upgrade, create a missing lightweight entry and complete affected structure, rule, and navigation migration under [Lifecycle](protocol.md#lifecycle); a new index over obsolete rules or reading routes is not completion. An explicit user-requested location takes precedence and must be directly identified by both navigation entries. This standardizes experience discovery, not every document location; ordinary tasks do not restart adoption.

Missing content can be marked unknown with a useful next action; it need not block authorized work. A complete knowledge structure does not prove team readiness. Before first professional delegation or adopting team mode, follow [Coordination](protocol.md#coordination) to reuse known visible professional ownership and, where a team must be established, complete all six visible roles in one setup within creation authority. Reuse accepted tasks and the current lead, keep unrelated roles on standby, and distinguish actual creation, readiness, and assignment. Historical partial arrangements remain truthfully recorded; do not duplicate tasks, equate read-version evidence with completeness, or infer creation authority from this template.

## First adoption or takeover

Use judgment about order, depth, and parallelism. Produce these outcomes, rather than a mandatory sequence of reports:

- **A reliable current picture:** recover the goal, applicable authority, existing role instances, effective plans, code/version state, usable artifacts, meaningful evidence, unfinished work, and applicable lessons. Synchronize the established role's actual visible task title under [Identity and authority](protocol.md#identity-and-authority), preserving explicit user choices and task history. Separate confirmed facts, supported inferences, and missing history. Use available relevant conversations when permitted; do not claim to know inaccessible decisions.
- **An integrated structure:** map all required areas to real homes and distinguish common rules, specific role responsibilities, contract meaning, methods, evidence, and current assignments under [Project knowledge](protocol.md#project-knowledge). Resolve competing texts and check the routes from AGENTS, TEAM, the overview, and experience to each responsibility's authoritative role card. Preserve useful structure, unique work, and historical reasons; establish actual relationships rather than filling templates from guesses.
- **An executable continuation:** carry continuing requirements and applicable lessons into prioritized outcomes, responsible owners, dependencies, needed acceptance evidence, and material owner choices. When execution is authorized, use the arrangement and ownership rules in [Coordination](protocol.md#coordination): team work routes affected outcomes through actual accepted tasks with verified receipt/access; suitable solo work proceeds with applicable quality and verification requirements. Advance that authorized work; a completed knowledge structure does not replace team dispatch or substantive continuation. Show a usable entry when one exists, and distinguish implemented, verified, deployed, and owner-accepted status.

Before reporting adoption complete, check that a successor can navigate from the entry to current facts, decisions, remaining work, and lessons; map links resolve; sampled consequential claims match their evidence; and unresolved conflicts are explicit. Check the claims necessary for this takeover, without turning it into a full product retest.

Reorganizing project knowledge does not imply redesigning working software. Assess technical restructuring on its own benefits, costs, dependencies, and authority. Unresolved areas should not stop unrelated authorized work. No separate takeover dossier is required when the overview and existing records already provide these outcomes.

## Project rules and methodology boundaries

This methodology defines the requested organizational and knowledge skeleton. A project supplies its facts, domain requirements, technical constraints, and scoped decisions; incidental rules from the project used to develop or test this skill must not become universal package requirements.

Inspect the applicable AGENTS.md chain, current request, and resource permissions against their original sources, scope, and stage; verify unclear restrictions before acting. Record temporary arrangements as temporary and preserve explicit continuing single-agent or no-delegation decisions. Use [Coordination](protocol.md#coordination) to judge result coverage, organizational mode, creation authority, and current role activation, including affected upgrade decisions; compliance with a restriction alone does not establish fitness for changed work. Reuse valid judgments and update superseded rules, role responsibilities, and reading routes only within current authority, retaining dated reasons. Keep common rules in AGENTS, specific continuing responsibilities in role cards, current work in the overview, and history in its records. Neither skill invocation nor expanded work grants missing task-creation authority.

Follow actual instruction priority. The owner's current explicit adoption and scope can supersede their old workflow preferences without another confirmation; do not silently drop the requested skeleton because an old template used a different process. Record consequential supersession with its source. Preserve constraints and stop decisions still valid for their objects. If a conflict genuinely cannot be resolved from authority, scope, and evidence, raise that specific decision while continuing unaffected work. A skill cannot override higher-priority platform instructions.

Verify shared project access and the actual target directory for the roles involved. A same-project label can still refer to separate worktrees; arrange separate workspaces deliberately and keep shared authoritative records reachable.

## Project knowledge and native memory

Project files and versioned evidence are the maintained basis for team delivery, whether platform memory is enabled or disabled. Keep retrieval active: read relevant sources at intake, assignment, consequential decisions, and handoff; update affected records after decisions and results.

Native memories, conversation summaries, and recalled prior-project experience can help locate useful context. Treat them as fallible recall with a source and applicability, not as a new authorization, current requirement, or proof of delivery. Verify consequential recollections against the current user request, effective project records, and actual evidence. A remembered workflow from another project must not silently replace this project's adopted methodology.

Native memory is platform-managed generated state and may update asynchronously. Do not use project cleanup to rewrite that store, copy it wholesale into the project, or toggle its settings. Use supported platform controls when explicitly requested. Keep the current native-memory configuration unchanged during ordinary skill installation or project adoption. Do not assume enabling it causes project files to be read automatically or preserves every conversation.

When memory and records disagree, resolve the dated sources and authority. Approved requirements define intended behavior; code and runtime describe actual behavior. Mark unresolved history rather than fabricating an agreement or altering requirements to make the implementation look correct.
