## Project coordination

Record the owner's requested visible-task/access arrangement, authorization for creation and coordination, the actual dispatch/receipt channel, and shared project location. Where applicable, point to existing cross-project professional ownership and its result/acceptance interface; it does not establish either project's full team. A shared file does not by itself wake another task.

The lead manages routine assignments, feedback, and rework. Roles may consult through authorized channels within the common protocol's visible-ownership and delegation boundaries; decisions and results reach the lead and common records. Only affected roles participate in each change. Keep authoritative write ownership clear and serialize dependent contract adoption.

The owner gives goals, makes material choices, and performs final experience acceptance. Team members choose suitable methods within their scope, question consequential assumptions, and propose worthwhile improvements across role boundaries. The lead brings unresolved material tradeoffs to the owner with a recommendation and practical consequences. Use the common package protocol rather than reproducing an entire workflow here.
