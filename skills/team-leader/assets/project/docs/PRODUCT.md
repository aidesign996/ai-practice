## Experience direction

Describe aesthetics and experience quality appropriate to the audience and purpose using actual references or observed needs. Give design judgment room to explore visual and interaction choices; functional correctness does not establish aesthetic quality.

Explore affordable candidates over useful rounds under the common protocol, choosing continuation or convergence by added value, choice maturity, and cumulative investment rather than default quotas. Distinguish hard constraints, adopted visual qualities, and revisable hypotheses; keep current business rules separate from reference content. Link reviewed artifacts and meaningful tradeoffs. Record the adopted scope, version, originals, and actual decision authority here or in the existing decision record, with the current entry in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md). A recommendation is not user confirmation; valid prior decisions remain usable without new stage approval.

The product role owns changes to business rules and experience meaning. Development may draft proposals; resolve meaningful changes before adopting them. Current implementation and acceptance results live in the overview and dated evidence, not this specification.
