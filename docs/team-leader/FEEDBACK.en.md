# Share your experience

English · [繁體中文](FEEDBACK.zh-Hant.md) · [简体中文](FEEDBACK.md) · [Back to Team Leader](ARTICLE.en.md)

You are welcome to use Team Leader, ask questions and suggest changes. I will keep improving it using real experiences. A few sentences are enough; you do not need to know systems theory or diagnose a technical cause first.

## Write it in your own words

Comment directly below the web article. The original feedback page shows the same conversation, with no separate fields or required template. You might describe what you wanted to do, what happened, and what would work better. Share whatever you know; experiences that worked well are welcome too.

For example: “I asked the leader to continue revising a page, but it only replied with a plan. I had to ask again. I would prefer it to continue within the existing authorization, and explain the specific blocker when it needs my decision.”

A version, tool name or screenshot with private information removed may help, but you do not need to prepare a technical report. English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese are all welcome.

[Comment below the article](article-preview.html#comments) · [View the discussion on GitHub](https://github.com/aidesign996/ai-practice/discussions)

All three language editions share one public discussion. Read, comment and reply within the article. The first time you post, sign in with GitHub and authorize giscus. Published comments are stored publicly in GitHub Discussions; remove private or confidential information. You can read without an account and join the conversation after registering.

> I was working on… and wanted… . What happened was… , which meant… . It would help if… . (It is fine if you do not know the solution.)

## How I will use the feedback

I will organize the feedback with the skill maintainer, understand the situation and original goal, then check what actually happened and distinguish a local need from a reusable improvement. After a change, I will return to the scenario to check it and explain the result and limitations. You and other users can continue in the same discussion.

Every suggestion deserves to be understood, but not every suggestion requires a new universal rule or release. Feedback helps the method fit real work.
