New to Team Leader? Start with your question.

Team Leader is an AI team working method packaged as a Skill. You set the goal and important tradeoffs; the lead organizes work, checks results, coordinates with other leads, and brings relevant experience into the next decision.

01

How can AI keep a project on course?

A finished step does not mean the whole result meets the goal. The lead compares real outputs with what you asked for, coordinates corrections, and checks the revised result.

See goals, roles & feedback →
02

Can project leads coordinate without me relaying messages?

In the sharing example, the article lead contacted the Skill developer to clarify design and usage conditions. Each kept responsibility for its own contribution. This needs communication tools and access to the relevant materials.

See cross-project collaboration →
03

How does experience help with the next task?

Keep the situation, choice, reasons, and result. When a similar problem arises, compare conditions before reusing the method. More bold text may help one article; less may help another. The goal is still clear reading.
