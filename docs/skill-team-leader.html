Team Leader

Give your AI project a team lead to coordinate the team and keep checking progress against your goal.

Version0.5.16
Primary environmentCodex · Astra
LicenseMIT

1. Install the complete Skill

In a supported AI tool with access to your project, you can say:

Please read https://github.com/aidesign996/ai-practice and help me install the complete Team Leader Skill.

2. Hand over the project in one sentence

After installation, select Team Leader or mention $team-leader in your project conversation, then say:

You're in charge of this project now. Please take it forward.

What happens next

The lead should read the available project context and goal, ask about essential missing requirements, and carry on with work that is clear and authorized. The short sentence does not guarantee automatic skill discovery in every tool.

Scope and usage

Designed primarily around Astra in Codex, especially for continuing work across product design, technical design, development, and review. Multi-role coordination can use substantial quota. The lead typically starts at XHigh while specialist effort is chosen for the task and supported runtime settings. Simple one-off tasks can stay simple.

Sol has partial specialist-role experience, but no comparable all-Sol team evaluation. Full installation-to-delivery validation on a clean machine or new account and long-term outcome measurement remain incomplete.
